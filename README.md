# Civ 6 Development Calculator

A prototype of a Civilization VI city-development calculator: it generates a
Civ 6-style hex map (with natural wonders), lets you found cities, place tile
improvements, districts and buildings, research the tech & civic trees, run a
government with policy cards, and simulates yields, growth, cultural border
expansion and production over turns — assuming you are **alone on the map**
with **no random events**.

Stage 1 delivered the map/yield/city/turn engine; stage 2 added natural
wonders, cultural border growth (plus tile purchase) and tech/civic trees with
governments & policies.

> Tip: `npm run preview:map [seed]` renders a generated map to `map-preview.png`
> headlessly (no browser needed) and prints terrain stats plus a scripted
> 60-turn playthrough — handy for checking generator tweaks.

## Running

```bash
npm install
npm run dev          # dev server at http://localhost:5173
npm test             # engine unit tests (vitest)
npm run build        # type-check + production build into dist/
npm run preview:map  # headless: map stats + scripted playthrough + map-preview.png
```

## What's simulated

- **Map generation** (deterministic per seed; Civ 6 map sizes from Duel 44×26 to
  Standard 84×54): continents from fractal noise, latitude climate bands
  (snow/tundra → desert belts → equatorial rainforest), hills & mountains, lakes,
  coast/ocean, features (woods, rainforest, marsh, floodplains, oasis, reef, ice)
  and ~35 bonus/luxury/strategic resources (toggleable).
- **Natural wonders** (toggleable): Crater Lake, Dead Sea, Galápagos, Great
  Barrier Reef, Pantanal, Uluru, Torres del Paine — multi-tile placement with
  spawn requirements, tile yields on passable wonders, adjacent-tile bonuses
  (Uluru +2🎭+2🙏, Galápagos +2🧪, Torres del Paine doubles terrain yields),
  +2 faith Holy Site adjacency, and full build/settle blocking.
- **Rivers run along hex edges** exactly like Civ 6 (not through tiles), generated by
  descending the hex-corner graph from highlands to the sea. River adjacency powers
  fresh-water housing, Commercial Hub adjacency and floodplains.
- **Tile yields**: terrain + hills + feature + resource + improvement, base Civ 6 values.
- **Improvements** (farm, mine, quarry, lumber mill, pasture, camp, plantation,
  fishing boats, oil well) with placement validation; instant and free (builders are
  not modeled in stage 1). Improved luxuries grant amenities (+1 to the 4 neediest
  cities per unique luxury).
- **Cities**: 3-tile minimum spacing; founding claims the center + first ring
  only (like Civ 6); free-worked center tile floored at 2🍞/1⚙; citizens work
  tiles chosen by a per-city focus (or manual tile locks); each citizen eats
  2🍞 and contributes 0.7🧪/0.3🎭.
- **Cultural border growth**: each city's culture fills an expansion box
  (cost 20 + 10·n^1.1) and claims the best adjacent tile out to 5 rings
  (closer > resources > yields, Civ 6-ish priorities); tiles can also be
  bought with gold (4× the culture cost, Land Surveyors discount applies),
  sharing the same escalating counter. Working/districting still caps at 3 rings.
- **Growth**: Civ 6 formula `15 + 8(P−1) + (P−1)^1.5`, with housing (fresh water /
  coastal / aqueduct / buildings / +0.5-per-farm-type improvements) and amenity
  tiers (Ecstatic…Unhappy) modifying growth and non-food yields.
- **Districts** (all base-game common districts incl. Aqueduct & Neighborhood) with
  real adjacency rules (Campus: +1/mountain, +0.5/rainforest…, Harbor: +2 city
  center +1/sea resource, Commercial Hub: +2 river, IZ: +1/mine&quarry, +0.5 per
  adjacent district everywhere), placement constraints (Harbor on coastal water,
  Aqueduct between center and water source, Encampment away from center, nothing on
  floodplains/oasis) and the 1-per-3-population specialty-district limit.
- **Buildings**: the full common chains (Monument→Sewer, Library→Research Lab,
  Shrine/Temple, Amphitheater→Broadcast Center, Market→Stock Exchange,
  Lighthouse→Seaport with the Shipyard's "production = harbor adjacency" rule,
  Workshop→Power Plant and Zoo/Stadium with 6-tile **regional** effects,
  Barracks|Stable→Military Academy, Arena). Capital gets a free Palace.
- **Tech & civic trees**: compact (22 techs / 28 civics) but with real Civ 6
  names, era structure, prereq shapes and gating — districts, buildings,
  improvements and feature removal all unlock the way they do in the game
  (Campus = Writing, Theater Square = Drama & Poetry, hill farms = Civil
  Engineering, chopping woods = Mining, …). One tech and one civic research at
  a time, fed by empire science/culture, with overflow and cheapest-first
  auto-pick when nothing is selected. Boosts included: Apprenticeship &
  Industrialization mines +1⚙, Feudalism/Replaceable Parts farm-adjacency food.
- **Governments & policies**: 10 governments (Chiefdom → Democracy/Communism/
  Fascism) with Civ 6-style slot layouts (military/economic/diplomatic/wildcard)
  and 19 policy cards — adjacency doublers (Natural Philosophy, Scripture,
  Town Charters, Craftsmen, …), building-yield boosters (Rationalism, Free
  Markets, Simultaneum, Grand Opéra), housing/amenity cards (Insulae, Medina
  Quarter, Liberalism, New Deal), Urban Planning, God King, Land Surveyors,
  Veterancy (+30% Encampment production). Slot-kind legality and card
  uniqueness enforced; switching re-seats compatible cards.
- **Turns**: per-city production queues with overflow; Next turn / +10 buttons;
  empire accumulators for science/culture/faith/gold. A **Sandbox** toggle makes all
  construction instant and free *and ignores research gating* for pure layout
  experiments.
- **Save/load** to browser localStorage; full game state is plain JSON (stage-1
  saves migrate automatically).

## Known simplifications (stage 1)

These are deliberate; the engine is data-driven so most are easy to revisit:

- Numbers are *eyeballed-but-close* to base Civ 6 — see `src/data/*.ts` to tweak
  any yield, cost or rule constant.
- The trees are compact (pure-military/filler nodes trimmed); no eurekas or
  inspirations; district costs stay flat (no game-progress cost scaling).
- No diplomatic policy cards exist (nothing to be diplomatic with) — diplomatic
  slots sit idle; Veterancy is the only military card. Some government inherent
  bonuses are eyeballed stand-ins where the real one needs unmodeled systems.
- Policy swaps and government changes are free anytime (Civ 6 charges gold or
  ties them to civic completions); no anarchy or legacy bonuses.
- Border-growth tile picking approximates Civ 6's priorities; no tile swapping
  between cities.
- No built wonders, great people, specialists, appeal, religion beliefs, trade
  routes, city-states, barbarians, units, combat, maintenance costs, or disasters.
- Neighborhood housing is a flat +4 (appeal isn't modeled).
- Citizen auto-assignment is a simple weighted greedy (use focus + tile locks for
  fine control).
- No world wrap; map edges are hard borders.

## Roadmap ideas (later stages)

1. Built wonders (incl. adjacency interactions) and great people.
2. Eurekas/inspirations and game-progress district cost scaling.
3. Import real map data extracted from a Civ 6 game via a mod (the `GameMap` JSON
   format in `src/core/types.ts` is the target interchange shape).
4. Optimization tooling: "best campus spot" suggestions, full build-order solving.

## Code layout

```
src/core/   DOM-free engine: hex math (incl. edge/vertex graph for rivers),
            mapgen, yields, placement rules, city stats, game state & turns
src/data/   all game-rule data: terrains, features, resources, improvements,
            districts, buildings, constants & formulas
src/ui/     canvas renderer + DOM panels
tests/      vitest suites for hex math, mapgen invariants, yields, game rules
scripts/    headless map preview / engine smoke test (writes map-preview.png)
```
